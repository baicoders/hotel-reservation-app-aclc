# Booking rules: check in and check out dates, total of rate times nights, no double booking inside the transaction

Cycle: 20260927-15179b
Started: 2026-09-27T21:17:35.627Z

## Goal
Apply the booking rules of DESKTOP_APPLICATION_REQUIREMENTS.txt section 6, which decision 0001 put in scope: guests choose check in and check out dates instead of a hard coded one night stay, check out must be after check in, the total is the nightly rate times the number of nights, the payment must equal that total, and availability is rechecked inside the booking transaction against overlapping active reservations so a room cannot be double booked. Also stop the booking form from crashing without a room and from opening for a room that cannot be booked. Cycle 14 of 18 in the UI/UX overhaul; the booking form redesign (Screen 7) follows in cycle 15 to stay under the diff limit.

## Approach
Project docs: decision 0001; section 6 rules (valid room, guest, dates and amount; payment equals the calculated total; check out after check in; reuse guests by email; one transaction; recheck availability inside it) and the recommended improvements (selected dates, rate times nights, transaction, recheck).

Rung 2: the existing `DB::transaction` with `lockForUpdate` on the room stays; the overlap check and the dates go inside it. Validation uses Laravel's `after_or_equal:today` and `after:check_in_date` rules.

Chosen and rejected:
- Chosen: two reservations overlap when one starts before the other ends and ends after the other starts, counting confirmed, checked in and pending reservations; checking out on the day the next guest checks in is allowed. Revisable.
- Chosen: a booking still marks the room unavailable, as SYSTEM_DOCUMENTATION.txt documents, so the public browser keeps listing only available rooms. Consequence to raise with the owner: a room booked for a future week also disappears from the browser for tonight. Rejected for now: per date availability in the browser, a larger change to the room model.
- Chosen: stays of at most 30 nights. Revisable.
- Chosen: the amount must equal the total to the cent; the error states the expected total. Rejected: accepting overpayment, since the receipt no longer shows change.
- Chosen: the booking form without a room_id redirects to the room browser; for a room that is not available it redirects to the room page with an error.
- Chosen: the current form gains the two date fields and the amount label now; its full redesign is cycle 15.

## Out of scope
Booking form redesign and live total (cycle 15). Receipt redesign and printing (cycle 16). Status changes after booking (cycle 17).
