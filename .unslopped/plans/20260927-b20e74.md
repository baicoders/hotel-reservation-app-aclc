# Availability by dates: bookings leave room status alone, check in and out drive it, only maintenance blocks booking

Cycle: 20260927-b20e74
Started: 2026-09-27T22:00:57.602Z

## Goal
Apply decision 0002: a booking never changes the room's status, and only maintenance blocks booking a room; dates decide the rest through the overlap check. Checking in marks the room unavailable; checking out or cancelling marks it available again unless another guest is still checked in or the room is in maintenance. This also delivers the planned cancellation cycle (section 6: cancellation releases the room when no other active reservation conflicts) and the cycle 7 review note that status changes flipped maintenance rooms. Cycle 15 of 19 in the UI/UX overhaul.

## Approach
Project docs: decision 0002 (room status is physical state; bookings leave it alone), decision 0001, section 6 rules (a room cannot be booked if unavailable, now meaning in maintenance or overlapping; check in and check out set their dates; cancellation releases the room when no other active reservation conflicts).

Rung 2: the existing transaction, overlap check and status update action stay; only the conditions around room status change.

Chosen and rejected:
- Chosen: booking is refused only for rooms in maintenance; an occupied (unavailable) room can be booked for later dates. The booking form and the guest and staff room pages offer booking unless the room is in maintenance.
- Chosen: status update rules. checked_in sets the room unavailable unless it is in maintenance. checked_out and cancelled set it available only when it is unavailable and no other reservation of that room is checked in. pending and confirmed leave the room alone. Rejected: the old rule that set rooms available or unavailable on every change, which also flipped rooms in maintenance.
- Chosen: deleting a guest frees only rooms that guest was checked in to, with the same two exceptions.
- Chosen: existing tests that encoded the old rule change with it, and the plan says which: BookingTest (booking marks the room unavailable becomes booking leaves it alone) and GuestListTest (the released room comes from a checked in reservation, not a confirmed one). No test is deleted.
- Chosen: no data migration; staff reset any room left unavailable by an old future booking (room 102 in the dev data, reported to the owner).

## Out of scope
Date search in the room browser and passing dates to the booking form (cycle 16). Booking form redesign (cycle 17). Receipt (cycle 18).
