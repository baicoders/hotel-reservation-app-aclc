## Goal
Close the gaps against DESKTOP_APPLICATION_REQUIREMENTS.txt that cycles 2 and 6 left. Screen 9: the reservations list shows the reservation ID and filters by status, date, guest and room. Screen 4: the dashboard lists the latest rooms with a count and offers a Refresh action. Cycle 9 of 16 in the UI/UX overhaul.

## Approach
Project docs: decision 0001; Screen 9 (Reservation ID, filter by status, date, guest, room) and Screen 4 (latest reservation, guest and room counts and lists, Refresh, empty states).

Rung 2: the filters extend the existing `when()` query and `withQueryString()` pager in ReservationController; the dashboard reuses its panel markup.

Chosen and rejected:
- Chosen: one GET filter form with Status (select), Guest (name or email), Room (number) and Date. Rejected: keeping the status links alongside a form, since the links would drop the other filters.
- Chosen: Date means "staying that day": check in on or before the date and check out on or after it. Revisable.
- Chosen: an invalid date is ignored instead of erroring, matching how an unknown status is ignored.
- Chosen: the dashboard keeps "Rooms not available" (added after the cycle 2 review) and adds "Latest rooms" with the five newest rooms and their status; the panels sit under a "Latest activity" heading.
- Chosen: Refresh is a link that reloads /dashboard, with "Updated at" showing the load time. Rejected: automatic polling, which the page does not need.
