# Receipt: Screen 8 confirmation after a redirect, print and save as PDF, book another room, no false email claim

Cycle: 20260928-2502da
Started: 2026-09-28T00:58:37.816Z

## Goal
Rebuild the booking receipt to DESKTOP_APPLICATION_REQUIREMENTS.txt Screen 8: guest, room and payment summaries, a reservation confirmation, Print receipt, Save as PDF, and Book another room or go home. Show it after a redirect instead of from the booking POST, so refreshing the receipt cannot submit the booking again, and remove the claim that a confirmation email was sent, since the app sends none. Cycle 18 of 19 in the UI/UX overhaul.

## Approach
Project docs: decision 0001; Screen 8 (guest summary, room summary, payment summary, reservation confirmation, print receipt, save or export receipt, start another reservation or home), section 6 (receipt data matches the persisted reservation and payment), the section 12 print receipt feature, and the dialog rule on duplicate submissions.

Rung 4: printing and PDF export use the browser's own print dialog (`window.print()` and its Save as PDF destination) with a print stylesheet through Tailwind's `print:` variant; no PDF library.

Chosen and rejected:
- Chosen: after booking, store the receipt data in the visitor's session and redirect to GET /guest/receipt, which reads it. The stay, room and payment come from the saved reservation and payment; the contact details are the ones the visitor typed, so the cycle 14 privacy fix holds. Rejected: a signed URL per reservation, which would need the stored guest record and bring the privacy leak back.
- Chosen: the receipt stays available for the rest of the session and is replaced by the next booking; opening /guest/receipt without one redirects to the room browser.
- Chosen: the confirmation number is the reservation id shown as "#" plus six digits.
- Chosen: the email sentence is removed. Rejected: sending mail, which needs a mail service the owner has not set up.

## Out of scope
Sending confirmation emails. Removing the unused old templates and updating SYSTEM_DOCUMENTATION.txt (cycle 19).
