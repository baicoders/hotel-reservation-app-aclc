## Goal
Rebuild the public room browser to DESKTOP_APPLICATION_REQUIREMENTS.txt Screen 5: available room cards with number, type, nightly price, availability status and first photo, View details and Reserve actions, plus the optional filter and sort. Add a public layout that the room details, booking and receipt pages reuse in later cycles. ## Approach
Project docs: decision 0001; Screen 5 (available room cards or table, room number, type, price per night, availability status, first photo thumbnail, View Details and Reserve actions, optional search, filter and sort) and section 6 (only available rooms may be shown in the public room browser).

Rung 2: the cards reuse `components/room-cards` (its guest mode gains Reserve), the pager and `when()` filters follow the staff rooms list.

Chosen and rejected:
- Chosen: a `layout.guest` with a slim header (ACLC mark, Rooms, Staff sign in) and a footer, extending `layout.app`. Rejected: reusing the home hero, which is too tall for task pages.
- Chosen: filter by room type from the types that available rooms actually have, and sort by price low to high, price high to low, or room number (default). Rejected: free text search, since guests pick by type and price, not room number.
- Chosen: 12 rooms per page; the old page stopped at 50 with no pager.
- Chosen: Reserve opens /guest/rooms/reserve?room_id={id}, the existing booking form.
