# Reservations list and detail: pagination, status filter, payment and guest details, linked room

Cycle: 20260927-029a7b
Started: 2026-09-27T01:44:53.667Z

## Goal
Make reservations workable at the front desk: a paginated list (today it stops at 50) with a status filter, dates and room numbers, and a detail page that shows the guest with contact details and a link, the room with its key tag and a link, the payment the controller already loads but never showed, and a clear status update. Cycle 6 of 10 in the UI/UX overhaul.

## Approach
Rung 5: Eloquent `paginate()` with `withQueryString()` and Laravel's bundled pager, as in the rooms list.

Chosen and rejected:
- Chosen: status filter as a row of links (All, Pending, Confirmed, Checked in, Checked out, Cancelled) with `aria-current` on the active one. Rejected: a select with a submit button, since one tap is enough for five fixed values.
- Chosen: the Cancelled filter matches both `cancelled` (written by the controller) and `canceled` (written by the factory).
- Chosen: 15 reservations per page, newest first, as before.
- Chosen: keep the status update form and its confirm dialog; the dialog now names the reservation and the new status. The status values and their room side effects stay as they are.
- Chosen: "No payment recorded" when a reservation has no payment row.

## Out of scope
Guest pages (cycle 7). Changing status rules or dates. Editing payments.
