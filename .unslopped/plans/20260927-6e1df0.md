## Approach
Project docs: decision 0001; Screen 13 (Create, Details, Edit and Delete actions), Screen 15 (image preview and removal before saving), dialog requirements (prevent duplicate submissions while a save is in progress), section 6 (deleting a room removes or archives the physical image files).

Rung 4: removing a picked photo rebuilds the file input's list with the native DataTransfer API, and double submit blocking uses the form submit event and the `disabled` attribute; no library.

Chosen and rejected:
- Chosen: Edit and Delete sit in the card footer above the stretched Details link, only on staff cards; the Delete confirmation states the room's reservation count, as on the room info page.
- Chosen: photo files are deleted from the public disk after the room row is deleted, so a failed delete never leaves a room pointing at missing files. Rejected: archiving the files, since nothing reads an archive.
- Chosen: every POST form disables its submit buttons and shows "Saving..." once it submits; forms sent through the confirm dialog are disabled when Confirm is pressed. GET search forms are left alone. The button comes back if the browser restores the page from its back cache.
- Chosen: a new `reservations` relation on Room for the card count.
