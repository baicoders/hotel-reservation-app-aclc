Cycle: 20260927-3e40e1

## Goal
Rebuild the booking form to DESKTOP_APPLICATION_REQUIREMENTS.txt Screen 7: a selected room summary, guest name, email, phone and address, payment amount, method and date, Submit and Cancel, and field level validation that keeps every entered value, including the payment method, which the old form lost after an error. The total follows the chosen dates. Cycle 17 of 19 in the UI/UX overhaul.

## Approach
Project docs: decision 0001; Screen 7 (selected room summary, name, email, phone, address, payment amount, payment method, payment date, submit, cancel or back, field level validation) and the dialog rules (show validation failures without losing entered values, prevent duplicate submissions, already global since cycle 10); decision 0002 (dates decide availability).

Rung 2: the page uses the public layout, the key tag and the shared field classes; the inline error pattern and the `inline-errors` section come from the room forms and login.

Chosen and rejected:
- Chosen: the amount field is read only and holds the total for the chosen dates, computed on the server when the form opens and updated in the browser when the dates change; the server still checks it. Rejected: a typed amount, which made guests do the arithmetic and fail validation.
- Chosen: the summary card shows the room, nightly rate, nights and total, and repeats the total next to Submit.
- Chosen: Cancel returns to the room page with the dates.
- Chosen: Submit reads "Book and pay"; the busy label reads "Booking...".
