# Room info page: photo gallery, room details with key tag, full reservation history, clear delete warning

Cycle: 20260927-cc508e
Started: 2026-09-27T00:59:51.767Z

## Goal
Rebuild the staff room info page: a photo gallery with previous and next controls, the room's details under a large key tag, its full reservation history with links, and a delete action whose warning says how many reservations go with the room. Cycle 5 of 10 in the UI/UX overhaul.

## Approach
Rung 4: the gallery uses native CSS scroll snap with two buttons that call `scrollBy`, so no carousel library is added.

Chosen and rejected:
- Chosen: list every reservation for the room, newest first, with a status badge and a link to its details. Rejected: keeping the confirmed only filter, which hid past, checked in and cancelled stays from staff. Revisable.
- Chosen: the delete confirm message states the reservation count, because the database cascades a room delete to its reservations and payments. Rejected: blocking the delete, which is a product decision outside this cycle.
- Chosen: a "Book this room" action that opens the existing guest reservation form for this room when it is available, for walk in guests.
- Chosen: the gallery shows "Photo 1 of 3" beside the buttons, and the buttons hide when a room has one photo or none.

## Out of scope
Deleting single photos. Removing photo files from disk when a room is deleted (logged as debt). Reservation and guest pages (cycles 6 and 7).
