# Search rooms by dates: browser lists rooms free for the chosen stay and passes the dates to room details and the booking form

Cycle: 20260927-52bbb0
Started: 2026-09-27T22:34:32.764Z

## Goal
Finish decision 0002 on the guest side: the public room browser searches by check in and check out dates (tonight by default) and lists rooms not in maintenance that have no overlapping stay, and the chosen dates carry through to the room page and the booking form. The room page warns when the room is already booked for those dates. Cycle 16 of 19 in the UI/UX overhaul.

## Approach
Project docs: decision 0002 (the browser lists rooms not in maintenance with no overlapping reservation for the dates), Screen 5 (optional search, filter and sort), Screen 6 (clear unavailable state), section 6 (only available rooms in the public browser).

Rung 5: Eloquent `whereDoesntHave('reservations', ...)` expresses "no overlapping stay" with the Room relation added in cycle 10; the overlap condition matches the booking check.

Chosen and rejected:
- Chosen: dates arrive as `check_in` and `check_out`; missing ones default to today and tomorrow. Invalid dates (past check in, check out not after check in, more than 30 nights) show an error above the results and fall back to the defaults. Rejected: an empty page on bad dates, which leaves the guest with nothing to act on.
- Chosen: guest cards show the badge "Available" for the chosen dates, not the room's current physical status, since a room occupied tonight can be free next week.
- Chosen: card links, the room page's Reserve link and the booking form carry the dates as query parameters; the booking form fills its date fields from them unless old input exists.
- Chosen: the room page with dates that overlap an existing stay shows "This room is already booked for some of these dates." with a link back to the browser for those dates, and no Reserve.
