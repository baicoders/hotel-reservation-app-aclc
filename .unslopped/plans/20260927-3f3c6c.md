## Goal
Rebuild the public entry pages to DESKTOP_APPLICATION_REQUIREMENTS.txt. Rung 5: Laravel's `guest` middleware already redirects signed in users to /dashboard, so the login routes only gain that middleware.

Chosen and rejected:
- Chosen: the home hero keeps public/aclc_building_img.jpg under a lobby teal overlay, with the title in the display face and Reserve a room (brass) and Staff sign in (outline) buttons. Manage keeps its target, /dashboard, which sends signed out visitors to the login form.
- Chosen: the footer shows the version from a new `app.version` setting read from APP_VERSION, and hides the version when it is not set. Rejected: inventing a version number.
- Chosen: the failed login error is keyed to `username` and shown under the field with `role="alert"`; the typed username is kept. The message text stays as it was.
- Chosen: the login form shows "Signing in..." while it submits, using the busy label from cycle 10.
- Chosen: login rate limiting is left for the owner to decide, since it is a security policy; logged as debt.
