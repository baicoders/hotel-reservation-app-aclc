## Goal
Rebuild the staff guest list to DESKTOP_APPLICATION_REQUIREMENTS.txt Screen 11 (name, email, phone, address, created date, Open details and Delete actions), with pagination (today it stops at 50) and a name or email search, and make deleting a guest release the rooms their active reservations held. Also records decision 0001 on following the requirements documents. Cycle 7 of 16 in the UI/UX overhaul.

## Approach
Project docs: decision 0001 makes DESKTOP_APPLICATION_REQUIREMENTS.txt sections 4 and 6 the checklist. Screen 11 fixes the columns and actions; section 6 GuestService asks the app to define whether deleting a guest cascades or is blocked, and says releasing a room happens when no other active reservation needs it.

Rung 2: pagination reuses the `paginate()` and pager pattern of the rooms and reservations lists, and the Delete action reuses the confirm dialog.

Chosen and rejected:
- Chosen: deleting a guest still cascades to their reservations and payments, and the confirm message states the reservation count. Before the delete, rooms held by that guest's confirmed or checked in reservations go back to available, unless another guest's confirmed or checked in reservation holds the same room. Rejected: blocking deletion while reservations exist, which would leave staff no way to remove a guest record. Revisable.
- Chosen: the room release and the delete run in one transaction, so a failure leaves both untouched.
- Chosen: 20 guests per page, newest first; the search matches name or email.
- Chosen: on phones the table scrolls inside its card, as on the reservations list.
